# Task board notes

`task_board.csv` contains the 221 numbered checklist items from Roadmap P0–P10. Priorities follow Roadmap §5: P0 marks correctness, safety, or phase-gating work; P1 marks required project outcomes; P2 marks usability or presentation improvements; P3 marks optional work. Since the roadmap assigns phase-level priorities rather than per-task priorities, task labels are an initial team proposal and should be reviewed by the decision owner.

Dependencies follow Roadmap §6: P1 depends on P0; P2 on P1; P3 and P4 on P2; P5 on P3 and P4; P6 and P7 on P5; P8 on P5 and P6; P9 on P6, P7, and P8; P10 on P9. Owners remain `<TBD>` and every task starts `Not Started` pending team assignment.
